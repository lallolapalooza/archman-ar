rrd_pdpcalc(1)

الحزمة: rrdtool

التراخيص: GPL-2.0-or-later

denotes 1000000005. We choose the type of the data source as gauge because original values will be treated as rates, no additional transformation is needed, see this article http://rrdtool.vandenbogaerdt.nl/process.php for detail.

05: 5.2 = (8*3+1*2)/5

10: 5.5 = (1*1+6*9)/10

15: the same as the previous one

20: 6.6 = (6*2+7*3)/5

25: 1.73333 = (7+4+3+1*12)/15

...

45: nan, as the last value is at 42,which does not trigger the calculation for PDP(41-45)

50: nan, why this unknown PDP is shown is explained in this article https://oss.oetiker.ch/rrdtool/tut/rrdtutorial.en.html

ملخص

كل ما قيل، آمل أن تحصل على فهم واضح للسحر الحسابي الداخلي لـ PDPs.
