There are multiple ways to produce headers and footers. One is to\n define the strings LH, CH, and RH to set the left, center, and right headers, respectively; and LF, CF, and\n RF to set the left, center, and right footers. This approach suffices\n for documents that do not distinguish odd- and even-numbered pages.

\n

Another method is to call macros that set headers or footers for\n odd- or even-numbered pages. Each such macro takes a delimited argument\n separating the left, center, and right header or footer texts from each\n other. You can replace the neutral apostrophes (') shown below with any\n character not appearing in the header or footer text. These macros are\n Berkeley extensions.\n
\n

\n
\n
.OH 'left'center'right'
\n
\n
.OF 'left'center'right'
\n
\n
.EH 'left'center'right'
\n
\n
.EF 'left'center'right'
\n
The OH and EH macros define headers for odd- (recto) and\n even-numbered (verso) pages, respectively; the OF and EF\n macros define footers for them.
\n
\n

With either method, a percent sign % in header or footer\n text is replaced by the current page number. By default, ms places no\n header on a page numbered “1” (regardless of its number\n format).

\n
