Description

These routines provide 64-bit integer interfaces to the BLAS routines. The 64-bit integer interfaces are useful for handling large matrices and vectors that might exceed the range of a 32-bit integer index.

The routines are named with a suffix of `_64` to distinguish them from the standard 32-bit integer interfaces. The parameters are the same as the standard BLAS routines, but with 64-bit integer types for dimensions and strides.

cblas_zher2_64

Performs the complex Hermitian rank-2 update.

Parameters:

  • layout: Specifies whether matrix A is stored in row-major or column-major order.
  • Uplo: Specifies whether A is an upper or lower Hermitian matrix.
  • N: The number of rows and columns of matrix A.
  • alpha: Pointer to the complex scalar that weights the contributions of X and Y.
  • X: Pointer to the first complex vector.
  • incX: The increment for the elements of X.
  • Y: Pointer to the second complex vector.
  • incY: The increment for the elements of Y.
  • A: Pointer to the complex Hermitian matrix.
  • lda: The leading dimension of A.

cblas_zhpr2_64

Performs the complex Hermitian rank-2 update on a packed matrix.

Parameters:

  • layout: Specifies whether matrix A is stored in row-major or column-major order.
  • Uplo: Specifies whether A is an upper or lower Hermitian matrix.
  • N: The number of elements to be packed in the first dimension of A.
  • alpha: Pointer to the complex scalar that weights the contributions of X and Y.
  • X: Pointer to the first complex vector.
  • incX: The increment for the elements of X.
  • Y: Pointer to the second complex vector.
  • incY: The increment for the elements of Y.
  • Ap: Pointer to the complex packed Hermitian matrix.
