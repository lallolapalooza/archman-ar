Detailed Description

LATDF performs a one-sided Jacobi SVD updating iteration. It is intended to be used in the context of the generalized Schur decomposition based algorithm for solving the Sylvester equation.

The generalized Schur decomposition of a matrix pair (A,B) is a pair of unitary matrices (Q, Z) such that

  H = Q*A*Z and K = Q*B*Z

where H and K are upper Hessenberg matrices.

The generalized Sylvester equation is of the form

  A*X + X*B = C

where A, B and C are square matrices. The algorithm used to solve this equation requires the solution of a sequence of smaller generalized Sylvester equations involving sub-matrices of A and B.

LATDF is part of the LAPACK routines for solving generalized Sylvester equations. Specifically, it's used in the context of updating the singular value decomposition (SVD) of a sub-matrix during the iterative process of solving the equation.

The routine iteratively updates a matrix T, which is related to the SVD of a sub-block of the original problem. The goal is to refine T until it satisfies certain conditions related to the desired accuracy of the solution to the Sylvester equation.

The parameters control the size of the sub-matrix being processed, the work space, and provide information about the progress and outcome of the iteration.

Note: This routine is part of a complex algorithm and is typically called by other LAPACK routines, not directly by the user.

Further Details:

This routine is part of the LAPACK implementation for solving generalized Sylvester equations. It performs a Jacobi SVD updating iteration on a sub-matrix to refine the solution. The specific details of the update depend on the context within the overall algorithm, typically involving updating the triangular matrix T which contains information about the SVD.
