NAME

lamswlq

applies a sequence of short-wide LQ Householder transformations to a general rectangular matrix

Detailed Description

This subroutine is the sequential short-wide LQ factorization interface.

It is used to apply Q or Q**H from ZLASWLQ to a general rectangular matrix C.

For more details of the overall algorithm, see the description of Sequential TSQR in Section 2.2 of [1].

[1] “Communication-Optimal Parallel and Sequential QR and LU Factorizations,” J. Demmel, L. Grigori, M. Hoemmen, J. Langou, SIAM J. Sci. Comput, vol. 34, no. 1, 2012
