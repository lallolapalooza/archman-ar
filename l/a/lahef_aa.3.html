!>
!> تقوم CLAHEF_AA بعامل لوحة من مصفوفة A الهرمية المعقدة باستخدام
!> خوارزمية Aasen. تتكون اللوحة من مجموعة من الصفوف NB من A
!> عندما يكون UPLO هو U ، أو مجموعة من الأعمدة NB عندما يكون UPLO هو L.
!>
!> لتصنيف اللوحة ، تتطلب خوارزمية Aasen الصف الأخير ، أو العمود ،
!> من اللوحة السابقة. يتم تعيين الصف الأول ، أو العمود ،
!> من A ليكون الصف الأول ، أو العمود ، من مصفوفة الهوية ،
!> والتي تستخدم لتصنيف اللوحة الأولى.
!>
!> يتم تخزين الصف J الناتج من U ، أو العمود J من L ، في
!> الصف (J-1) ، أو العمود ، من A (بدون الأقطار الوحدوية) ، بينما
!> يتم الكتابة فوق القطر الفرعي والقطر الفرعي لـ A بتلك الخاصة بـ T.
!>
!>

الغرض:

!>
!> DLASYF_AA provides a partial factorization of a general matrix A.
!>
!> It is expected that
!>
!>    A(1:N, J1:N) = A_panel * U_rest
!>
!> where A_panel is an NB-by-N matrix and U_rest is an N-by-N matrix
!> with U_rest(1:NB, 1:NB) = U, and U_rest(1:NB, NB+1:N) = U_top,
!> and U_rest(NB+1:N, NB+1:N) = I.
!>
!> The factorization is performed for a block of NB columns.
!>
!> UPLO = 'U' : A is upper triangular, the factorization is of the form
!>
!>    A = P * A_panel * U_rest
!>
!> where P is a permutation matrix.
!>
!> UPLO = 'L' : A is lower triangular, the factorization is of the form
!>
!>    A = P * A_panel * L_rest
!>
!> where P is a permutation matrix.
!>
!>

المعلمات

!>          = 'L':  Lower triangular storage
!>

J1

!>          J1 is INTEGER
!>          The column index of the first column of the panel
!>          in the matrix A.
!>

M

!>          M is INTEGER
!>          The number of rows in the matrix A. M >= 0.
!>

!>

subroutine zlahef_aa (character uplo, integer j1, integer m, integer nb, double complex, dimension( lda, * ) a, integer lda, integer, dimension( * ) ipiv, double complex, dimension( ldh, * ) h, integer ldh, double complex, dimension( * ) work)

ZLAHEF_AA

Purpose:

!>
!> ZLAHEF_AA factorizes a panel of a double precision complex hermitian matrix A using
!> the Aasen's algorithm. The panel consists of a set of NB rows of A
!> when UPLO is U, or a set of NB columns when UPLO is L.
!>
!> In order to factorize the panel, the Aasen's algorithm requires the
!> last row, or column, of the previous panel. The first row, or column,
!> of A is set to be the first row, or column, of an identity matrix,
!> which is used to factorize the first panel.
!>
!> The resulting J-th row of U, or J-th column of L, is stored in the
!> (J-1)-th row, or column, of A (without the unit diagonals), while
!> the diagonal and subdiagonal of A are overwritten by those of T.
!>
!>

Parameters

UPLO 
!>          UPLO is CHARACTER*1
!>          = 'U':  Upper triangle of A is stored;
!>          = 'L':  Lower triangle of A is stored.
!>

J1

!>          J1 is INTEGER
!>          The location of the first row, or column, of the panel
!>          within the submatrix of A, passed to this routine, e.g.,
!>          when called by ZHETRF_AA, for the first panel, J1 is 1,
!>          while for the remaining panels, J1 is 2.
!>

M

!>          M is INTEGER
!>          The dimension of the submatrix. M >= 0.
!>

NB

!>          NB is INTEGER
!>          The dimension of the panel to be facotorized.
!>

A

!>          A is DOUBLE COMPLEX array, dimension (LDA,M) for
!>          the first panel, while dimension (LDA,M+1) for the
!>          remaining panels.
!>
!>          On entry, A contains the last row, or column, of
!>          the previous panel, and the trailing submatrix of A
!>          to be factorized, except for the first panel, only
!>          the panel is passed.
!>
!>          On exit, the leading panel is factorized.
!>

LDA

!>          LDA is INTEGER
!>          The leading dimension of the array A.  LDA >= max(1,N).
!>

IPIV

!>          IPIV is INTEGER array, dimension (N)
!>          Details of the row and column interchanges,
!>          the row and column k were interchanged with the row and
!>          column IPIV(k).
!>

H

!>          H is DOUBLE COMPLEX workspace, dimension (LDH,NB).
!>
!>

LDH

!>          LDH is INTEGER
!>          The leading dimension of the workspace H. LDH >= max(1,M).
!>

WORK

!>          WORK is DOUBLE COMPLEX workspace, dimension (M).
!>
