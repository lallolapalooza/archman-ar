NAME

shseqr — compute the eigenvalues and Schur factorization of a real upper quasi-triangular matrix.

SYNOPSIS

    SUBROUTINE SHSEQR( JOB, COMPZ, N, ILO, IHI, H, LDH, WR, WI, Z, LDZ, WORK,
           LWORK, INFO )
        REAL(KIND=8)          H( LDH, * )
        INTEGER               INFO, IHI, ILO, LDH, LDZ, LWORK, N
        CHARACTER(LEN=1)      COMPZ, JOB
        REAL(KIND=8)          WORK( * )
        REAL(KIND=8)          WI( * )
        REAL(KIND=8)          WR( * )
        REAL(KIND=8)          Z( LDZ, * )

Function/Subroutine Documentation

This subroutine is part of the LAPACK package.

Purpose:

SHSEQR computes the eigenvalues and the Schur factorization of a real upper quasi-triangular matrix H. The matrix H is assumed to be in standard form, that is, H is upper quasi-triangular with side length 1 or 2 blocks on the diagonal.

This subroutine is intended for use by the inverse eigenvalue problem routines and the LAPACK driver routines.

Authors:

The version of the algorithm implemented in this subroutine is described in:

R. Braman, R. Byers and R. Mathias, The Multi-Shift QR Algorithm Part I: Maintaining Well Focused Shifts, and Level 3 Performance, SIAM Journal of Matrix Analysis, volume 23, pages 929--947, 2002.

R. Braman, R. Byers and R. Mathias, The Multi-Shift QR Algorithm Part II: Aggressive Early Deflation, SIAM Journal of Matrix Analysis, volume 23, pages 948--973, 2002.

This subroutine was written by:

K. Braman and R. Byers, University of Kansas, USA.
