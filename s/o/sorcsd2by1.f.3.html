sorcsd2by1.f(3)

sorcsd2by1 is a utility for managing the sorcsd2by1 service.

It can be used to start, stop, restart, and get the status of the service.

Usage:

sorcsd2by1 [command]

Commands:

start
Starts the sorcsd2by1 service.
stop
Stops the sorcsd2by1 service.
restart
Restarts the sorcsd2by1 service.
status
Gets the status of the sorcsd2by1 service.
