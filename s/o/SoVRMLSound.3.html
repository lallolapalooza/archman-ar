Detailed Description

The SoVRMLSound node defines an audible source within the virtual world. A sound node defines the sound source, its location, intensity, and how it attenuates over distance. It also defines how the sound source may be spatialized. The sound source itself is defined by the source field, which must be a SoVRMLAudioClip or SoVRMLMovieTexture node.

If the spatialize field is TRUE, the sound is spatialized relative to the viewer. If the spatialize field is TRUE and the viewer is located between the transformed inner and outer ellipsoids, the viewer's direction and the relative location of the Sound node should be taken into account during playback. Details outlining the minimum required spatialization functionality can be found in 7.3.4, Sound priority, attenuation, and spatialization (http://www.web3d.org/documents/specifications/14772/V2.0/part1/concepts.html#7.3.4). If the spatialize field is FALSE, then directional effects are ignored, but the ellipsoid dimensions and intensity will still affect the loudness of the sound. If the sound source is multi-channel (e.g., stereo), then the source should retain its channel separation during playback.
