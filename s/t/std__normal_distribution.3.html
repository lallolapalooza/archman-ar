Detailed Description

This class produces random numbers with a normal (Gaussian) distribution. The probability density function is:

f(x | μ, σ2) = (1 / sqrt(2πσ2)) * exp(- (x - μ)2 / (2σ2))

where μ is the mean and σ2 is the variance.
