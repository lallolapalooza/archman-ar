Detailed Description

The Fisher F distribution. This is the distribution of the ratio of two independent chi-squared distributions, each divided by their degrees of freedom. It is often used in analysis of variance.

The distribution is parameterized by two positive floating-point values, commonly denoted f1 and f2, representing the degrees of freedom of the two underlying chi-squared distributions.
