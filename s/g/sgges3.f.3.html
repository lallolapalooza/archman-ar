NAME

sgges3 - computes for the generalized eigenvalue problem A*x = lambda*B*x the generalized eigenvalues, and optionally generalized eigenvectors, of two general matrices A and B.

SYNOPSIS

 SUBROUTINE SGGES3(
                JOBVL, JOBVR, HOWMNY, SELECT,
                N, A, LDA, B, LDB,
                ALPHAR, ALPHAI, BETA,
                VL, LDVL, VR, LDVR,
                WORK, LWORK, BWORK, INFO )

            CHARACTER          JOBVL, JOBVR, HOWMNY
            INTEGER            N, LDA, LDB, LDVL, LDVR, LWORK, INFO
            REAL               A( LDA, * ), B( LDB, * ),
                               ALPHAR( * ), ALPHAI( * ), BETA( * ),
                               VL( LDVL, * ), VR( LDVR, * ), WORK( * )
            LOGICAL            SELECT( * ), BWORK( * )

Function/Subroutine Documentation

SGGSVD2 computes the singular value decomposition (SVD) of a real matrix A in the form W*D*Z**T, where S is an m x n matrix and Z is an n x n unitary matrix.

Purpose:

The purpose of SGGSVD2 is to compute the singular value decomposition of a real matrix A in the form A = U * S * V**T, where S is an m x n matrix, U is an m x m orthogonal matrix, and V is an n x n orthogonal matrix.

More precisely, it computes the SVD of A, and if requested, the SVD of B, such that:

        A = U * S * [0 V1* ]
                    [  V2* ]

        B = V * T * [I   0 ]
                    [ 0   0 ]

where U and V are orthogonal matrices.

Parameters:

JOBVL - CHARACTER*1.

>          = 'N':  Do not compute the left eigenvectors of A.
>          = 'V':  Compute the left eigenvectors of A.
>
>          In fact, VL and the columns of A corresponding to eigenvalues in
>          the selected subpencil of (A,B) are computed.
>
>          = 'B':  Compute the left eigenvectors of both A and B.
>
>          In fact, VL and the columns of A and B corresponding to eigenvalues
>          in the selected subpencil of (A,B) are computed.
>          = 'L':  Compute the left eigenvectors of A and the right eigenvectors
>                  of B.
>
>          In fact, VL and the columns of A and B corresponding to eigenvalues
>          in the selected subpencil of (A,B) are computed.

JOBVR - CHARACTER*1.

>          = 'N':  Do not compute the right eigenvectors of B.
>          = 'V':  Compute the right eigenvectors of B.
>
>          In fact, VR and the columns of B corresponding to eigenvalues in
>          the selected subpencil of (A,B) are computed.
>
>          = 'B':  Compute the right eigenvectors of both A and B.
>
>          In fact, VR and the columns of A and B corresponding to eigenvalues
>          in the selected subpencil of (A,B) are computed.
>          = 'R':  Compute the right eigenvectors of B and the left eigenvectors
>                  of A.
>
>          In fact, VR and the columns of A and B corresponding to eigenvalues
>          in the selected subpencil of (A,B) are computed.

HOWMNY - CHARACTER*1.

>          = 'A': compute all eigenvalues.
>          = 'S': compute selected eigenvalues.
>

SELECT - LOGICAL array, dimension (N).

>          If HOWMNY = 'S', SELECT specifies which eigenvalues to compute.
>          To select the j-th eigenvalue, set SELECT(j) to .TRUE..
>          If HOWMNY = 'A', SELECT is not referenced.
>

N - INTEGER.

>          The order of the matrices A and B. N >= 0.
>

A - REAL array, dimension (LDA, N).

>          On entry, the leading N x N part of this array must contain
>          the matrix A.
>          On exit, if JOBVL = 'V' or 'B' or 'L', VL contains the left
>          eigenvectors of A.
>          On exit, if JOBVR = 'V' or 'B' or 'R', VR contains the right
>          eigenvectors of A.
>

LDA - INTEGER.

>          The leading dimension of the array A. LDA >= max(1,N).
>

B - REAL array, dimension (LDB, N).

>          On entry, the leading N x N part of this array must contain
>          the matrix B.
>          On exit, if JOBVL = 'V' or 'B' or 'L', VL contains the left
>          eigenvectors of B.
>          On exit, if JOBVR = 'V' or 'B' or 'R', VR contains the right
>          eigenvectors of B.
>

LDB - INTEGER.

>          The leading dimension of the array B. LDB >= max(1,N).
>

ALPHAR - REAL array, dimension (N).

>          On exit, ALPHAR contains the real parts of the complex
>          eigenvalues.
>

ALPHAI - REAL array, dimension (N).

>          On exit, ALPHAI contains the imaginary parts of the complex
>          eigenvalues.
>

BETA - REAL array, dimension (N).

>          On exit, BETA contains the imaginary parts of the complex
>          eigenvalues.
>

VL - REAL array, dimension (LDVL, N).

>          If JOBVL = 'V' or 'B' or 'L', VL is an output array containing
>          the left eigenvectors of A and/or B.
>

LDVL - INTEGER.

>          The leading dimension of the array VL. LDVL >= 1.
>          If JOBVL = 'V' or 'B' or 'L', LDVL >= N.
>

VR - REAL array, dimension (LDVR, N).

>          If JOBVR = 'V' or 'B' or 'R', VR is an output array containing
>          the right eigenvectors of A and/or B.
>

LDVR - INTEGER.

>          The leading dimension of the array VR. LDVR >= 1.
>          If JOBVR = 'V' or 'B' or 'R', LDVR >= N.
>

WORK - REAL array, dimension (LWORK).

>          On exit, if LWORK = -1, WORK(1) contains the optimal LWORK.
>

LWORK - INTEGER.

>          The dimension of the array WORK. LWORK >= 1.
>          For good performance, LWORK must generally be larger.
>
>          If LWORK = -1, then a workspace query is assumed; the routine
>          only calculates the optimal size of the WORK array, returns
>          this value as the first entry of the WORK array, and no error
>          message related to LWORK is issued by XERBLA.
>

BWORK - LOGICAL array, dimension (N).

>          Not referenced if HOWMNY = 'A'.
>

INFO - INTEGER.

>          = 0:  successful exit
>          < 0:  if INFO = -i, the i-th argument had an illegal value.
>          = 1,...,N:
>                The QZ iteration failed.  (A,B) are not in Schur
>                form, but ALPHAR(j), ALPHAI(j), and BETA(j) should
>                be correct for j=INFO+1,...,N.
>          > N:  =N+1: other than QZ iteration failed in SLAQZ0.
>                =N+2: after reordering, roundoff changed values of
>                      some complex eigenvalues so that leading
>                      eigenvalues in the Generalized Schur form no
>                      longer satisfy SELCTG=.TRUE.  This could also
>                      be caused due to scaling.
>                =N+3: reordering failed in STGSEN.
>
