NAME

ztfsm - performs triangular factorization of a matrix and solves a linear system.

Function/Subroutine Documentation

  Purpose
  =======
  
  ZTFSM performs a triangular factorization of a matrix and solves a
  linear system. This routine is part of the LAPACK routines for
  performing triangular factorization of a matrix. It is designed to work
  with distributed matrices where the factorization is performed in a
  block-wise manner.
  
  The routine solves one of the matrix equations:
  
     op(A) * X = alpha * B
     or
     X * op(A) = alpha * B
  
  where op(A) is one of:
  
     op(A) = A   or   op(A) = A^T   or   op(A) = conjg(A^T)
  
  and X is an M-by-N matrix, A is a K-by-K triangular matrix, and B is
  an M-by-N matrix. ALPHA is a scalar.
  
  The parameter TRANSR is used to specify the layout of the triangular
  matrix A.
  
  Parameters
  ==========
  
  TRANS   (input) CHARACTER*1
          Specifies whether to apply the transpose of A or the conjugate
          transpose of A.
          'N': op(A) = A
          'T': op(A) = A^T
          'C': op(A) = conjg(A^T)
  
  DIAG    (input) CHARACTER*1
          Specifies whether the matrix A is unit triangular.
          'U': A is unit triangular
          'N': A is not unit triangular
  
  SIDE    (input) CHARACTER*1
          Specifies whether A is a left or right factor in the equation.
          'L': op(A) * X = alpha * B
          'R': X * op(A) = alpha * B
  
  UPLO    (input) CHARACTER*1
          Specifies whether A is upper or lower triangular.
          'U': A is upper triangular
          'L': A is lower triangular
  
  TRANSR  (input) CHARACTER*1
          Specifies the storage format of the triangular matrix A.
          'N': Normal storage format (e.g., upper triangular stored in
               upper triangular format).
          'C': Conjugate transpose storage format (e.g., upper triangular
               stored in lower triangular format).
  
  M       (input) INTEGER
          The number of rows of the matrix B and the left-hand side X.
          M >= 0.
  
  N       (input) INTEGER
          The number of columns of the matrix B and the left-hand side X.
          N >= 0.
  
  K       (input) INTEGER
          The number of columns of the left factor of A, and the number of
          rows of the right factor of A. K >= max(M,N) if SIDE = 'L',
          K >= N if SIDE = 'R'.
  
  ALPHA   (input) COMPLEX*16
          The scalar alpha.
  
  A       (input) COMPLEX*16 array, dimension (LDA, K)
          The triangular matrix.
  
  LDA     (input) INTEGER
          The leading dimension of the array A. LDA >= max(1, K).
  
  B       (input/output) COMPLEX*16 array, dimension (LDB, N)
          On entry, the right-hand side matrix.
          On exit, B is overwritten by the solution matrix X.
  
  LDB     (input) INTEGER
          The leading dimension of the array B. LDB >= max(1, M).
  
  
  Diagrammatic Representation of RFP A (Reduced Factorization Part)
  ===================================================================
  
  Consider a 5x5 matrix A.
  
  If UPLO = 'L' and TRANSR = 'N', the reduced factorization part (RFP)
  of A is stored in the lower triangle of A, and the upper triangle
  of A contains auxiliary information. The elements of RFP A are the
  conjugate transposes of the last two columns of AP lower.
  To denote conjugate we place -- above the element. This covers the
  case N odd and TRANSR = 'N'.
  
           RFP A                   RFP A
  
                                     -- --
          02 03 04                00 33 43
                                     --
          12 13 14                10 11 44
  
          22 23 24                20 21 22
          --
          00 33 34                30 31 32
          -- --
          01 11 44                40 41 42
  
  Now let TRANSR = 'C'. RFP A in both UPLO cases is just the conjugate-
  transpose of RFP A above. One therefore gets:
  
  
           RFP A                   RFP A
  
     -- -- --                   -- -- -- -- -- --
     02 12 22 00 01             00 10 20 30 40 50
     -- -- -- --                   -- -- -- -- --
     03 13 23 33 11             33 11 21 31 41 51
     -- -- -- -- --                   -- -- -- --
     04 14 24 34 44             43 44 22 32 42 52
