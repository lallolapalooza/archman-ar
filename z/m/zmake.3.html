DESCRIPTION

These subroutines are LAPACK diagnostic routines, intended to be called by the user only occasionally, to test the correctness of the LAPACK routines.

The routines zchk1 through zchk6 perform checks on the routines in the auxiliary, BLAS, and factorization & solution sections of the LAPACK library. They are called by the LAPACK testing programs to check the correctness of the LAPACK routines.
