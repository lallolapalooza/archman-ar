!>  This routine is the sequential two-stage version of the band divide and conquer
!>  routine for the real symmetric band matrix eigenvalue problem.
!>
!>  It is used to compute the eigenvalues and eigenvectors of a complex Hermitian
!>  positive definite band matrix A.
!>
!>  This routine is patterned after the LAPACK routines ZHEEV, ZHEGV, ZHEEVX, ZHEGVX,
!>  and ZHEBVD, ZHEGVD, ZHEBVX, ZHEGVX.
!>
!>  The algorithm performs the following steps:
!>  1. Reduce the band matrix to tridiagonal form.
!>  2. Solve the tridiagonal eigenproblem using a divide and conquer method.
!>  3. Form the eigenvectors of the original band matrix.
!>
!>  The routine requires two workspace arrays, WORK and RWORK.
!>  The dimensions of these arrays are N and N respectively.
!>
!>  The routine is not parallelized. It is intended to be used as a
!>  building block for parallel implementations of band eigensolvers.
!>
!>  The routine is also not thread-safe. It should not be called concurrently
