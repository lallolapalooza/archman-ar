NAME

zhseqr - The LAPACK routine zhseqr computes the eigenvalues and Schur decomposition of a general rectangular matrix.

SYNOPSIS

SUBROUTINE ZHSEQR( JOB, COMPZ, N, ILO, IHI, H, LDH, W, Z, LDZ, WORK, LWORK, INFO )

COMPLEX*16 H( LDH,*)

COMPLEX*16 Z(LDZ,*)

COMPLEX*16 WORK(LWORK)

Function/Subroutine Documentation

zhseqr is a subroutine within the LAPACK library designed for the numerical computation of eigenvalues and the Schur decomposition of a general rectangular matrix. It is particularly useful for dense matrices where an accurate and stable decomposition is required.

zhseqr is typically called by other LAPACK routines, such as DGEES and ZGEES, to perform the Schur decomposition of a general matrix. The Schur decomposition of a matrix $A$ is a factorization $A = Q Z Q^H$, where $Q$ is a unitary matrix and $Z$ is an upper triangular matrix. The diagonal elements of $Z$ are the eigenvalues of $A$.

The subroutine implements a sophisticated version of the QR algorithm, which is known for its numerical stability and efficiency. It incorporates techniques such as multi-shift QR iterations and aggressive early deflation to accelerate convergence and improve performance.
